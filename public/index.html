About DarKhwast

A darkhwast — a formal petition — for every citizen.

In Pakistan, a darkhwast is a typed, stamped, receipted petition. DarKhwast brings that idea online: anyone can describe a civic problem in plain language and get a properly addressed, professional complaint — without knowing which office to contact.

Complaints are classified with AI, drafted as formal letters, and emailed to the government department responsible for the citizen's city. Citizens stay anonymous by default and can track everything on a public page.

DarKhwast drafts, routes and tracks your complaint up to the department. Departments act on their own authority.
